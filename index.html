About Aishwarya

Based in Pune, India, Aishwarya Harishankar is an actress, model, and digital creator known for her expressive screen presence across multilingual entertainment. Holding a Bachelor’s degree in Psychology, she brings depth and emotional nuance to her performances, headlining chart-topping music videos like Mazya Sonya (20M+ views) and Atha Petha Azhakay (4.4M+ views), as well as intense thriller short films. Beyond the camera, she connects with an audience of over 1.4 million across social platforms, earning YouTube's prestigious Silver Creator Award. Multilingual and versatile, she continually explores impactful roles across cinema, music, and digital storytelling.
